## Country and city [K]

**Zelmava**, officially the **Zelmavian Workers' Republic** (*Zelmavska Delna Republika*, **ZDR**). Landlocked, nine million people, Austria across the south-western border. Capital **Kalenov** on the river **Oren**, 1.1 million; the art bible's three strata are Lom and the Embankment (imperial core), the ministry quarter, and **South Estate** (*Sidlisko Juh*). Official paper dates everything twice: `1989` and **`R.40`**, Year 40 of the Republic, founded 12 November 1949.

## The regime and its device [K]

The **Party of Working Unity** (*Partia Delnej Holny*, **PDH**); First Secretary **Mirek Dolnak**, in office since 1971, seventy-four. The state device is the **Register Mark**: a rectangular seal holding a filed rectangle with a tab — a card in a tray, seen from above. Official reading: "the record of the people is the people's shield." Everyone calls it **the Drawer**. "You'll go in the Drawer." "The Drawer is open" means someone is watching. It is printed in stamp red and nothing else is.

## How the six weeks are reckoned [K]

Every office and most kitchens hang the **Plan Calendar R.40**: one sheet per week, Monday to Sunday, Plan Weeks 1–52, a slogan and the week's production figure across the top, blank on the back (scrap paper; a torn-off sheet is how people pass notes). The game runs **Plan Week 40** (Monday 2 October) to **Plan Week 45**, closing Sunday **12 November, Day of the Republic**, the fortieth anniversary: parade on the Oren Embankment, the deadline every institution works toward.

## The Records Office [K/D]

The **State Records Service** (*Statna Karna Sluzba*, **SKS**) has two halves: the **Records Section** ("the paper side") and the **Operations Section** ("the corridor"). Vera's **District IV Records Office** occupies the banking hall of the old Lom Savings Bank: counters are reading desks, the vault is the restricted fond.

A dossier is a grey-green board folder, cloth spine, string tie, a gummed **tab** top right with the serial. Tab stripe red = active, blue = closed, no stripe = card only. Inside: form **K-1** cover sheet; every leaf numbered by hand in the corner; reports typed through two carbons — white original to central registry, **violet carbon** stays in district, pink to the handling officer. The **District Register** (the ledger) is bound, ink only, never erased: struck through and initialled. Serial scheme: `district/serial/year opened`, so **IV/4471/R.31** and **IV/7781/R.40**. Serials run in sequence; 7781 is one of this autumn's openings. "A complete ledger" means every serial has a folder and every folder its central carbon. Closed fonds are microfiched since R.36. [D] Twelve serials in R.38 are struck and initialled "Z". [H] They were informers transferred to Dolnak's personal office.

## The Ministry [K]

The **Ministry of Internal Order** (*Ministerstvo Vnutorneho Radu*, **MVR**), Oren Embankment 2 — **the Ribbon**, for its ribbon windows. Numbered doors, no nameplates. Central registry floors 3–4; the recorder room in the basement.

## The border [K]

**Brenava Crossing**, seventy kilometres south-west: road and rail bridge over the Oren into Austria. Garrison: **4th Battalion, Border Guard** (*Brenna Straz*, "the greens"), Brenava Post. The Tuesday international train from Vienna is inspected on the bridge at 14:10.

## Lom and Vrba Street [K]

District IV, **Lom** ("Old Quarry"): stucco tenements, gallery courtyards, chestnuts. **Vrba Street** (Willow Street) runs from Lom Square to the tram 11 loop. Number 9: see lore-building.md.

## What people queue for [K]

Meat on Thursdays; oranges in December; toilet paper when the lorry comes; the 6:00 kiosk because *Delnik* sells out as wrapping; the **Vienna call** at the Lom post office (booked Tuesday, three minutes); the **grey card** (exit permit); a telephone line (eight years); a flat (twelve); petrol on even-plate days.

## What the radio says [K]

**Radio Zelmava, Programme One**: 5:00 anthem and Plan bulletin, harvest figures, "the situation in neighbouring states", music at 21:00, the time signal on the hour. Its vocabulary tracks the weeks: "individual elements" (W40), "provocateurs" (W42), then "dialogue" or "order", depending on dossier state (narrative-director's call). After dark half the city tunes to the Vienna transmitter.

## What a phone tap sounds like [D]

The Kalenov exchange is electromechanical. A tapped line hums louder, gives a soft double click two seconds after the called party answers (the Ribbon basement relay), and stays open half a second after you hang up. Tapes are reel-to-reel, labelled by serial. People say "say good evening to the third one." *Flag to game-designer: an audible, learnable surveillance cue.*

## Five real-texture details per location

- **Records Office**: brass bank counter with a sliding grille; carbon paper reused until it shreds; a tea urn on a Plan Calendar; finger-cots in a dish; the vault's time-lock that still runs on the bank's 1912 clock.
- **The Ribbon**: door numbers stencilled, not signed; a paternoster lift; linoleum worn to the floor-plan of the queue; bakelite rotary phones with the dial locked; an ashtray on every windowsill.
- **Brenava Crossing**: raked sand strip; dog run; the stamp cabinet with twelve stamps and one ink; conscripts' boots re-soled with tyre rubber; the kiosk that sells only matches and the Party daily.
- **Vrba Street, Lom**: timed stair lights that click off after ninety seconds; coal-smoke sodium dusk; the gallery washing lines; a *Delnik* under every doormat; the tram 11 bell at the loop.
- **South Estate**: panel seams weeping rust; a dairy with a queue-number dispenser; balconies glazed with salvaged windows; one telephone booth per block; chalk on prefab concrete.
