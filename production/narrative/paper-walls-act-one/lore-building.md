## The house [K]

A four-storey stucco **gallery house** built in 1894 under the old Crown, in the Lom quarter. Street front: five windows wide, ochre stucco gone grey-green, a carriage passage with a wooden gate that is locked at 22:00 and opened by the caretaker. Behind it, a cobbled **courtyard** with one chestnut, the bins, a carpet-beating rail, and a cellar hatch. The upper floors are reached by one **stair** in the rear corner of the passage, and each floor opens onto an **open gallery** (*pavlac*) that runs along the courtyard side; rear flats are entered from the gallery, front flats from the landing.

Door numbers start at 11 because the Crown-era plan numbered the ground-floor shop, store, stable and lodge 1–10, and the housing cooperative never renumbered. Ground floor now: the carriage passage, the caretaker's **lodge** (a cubby with a hatch window onto the passage, by day Kastan's post), a closed cooperative repair shop, the cellar.

## Sightlines [K]

Kastan has three. From the **lodge hatch** he sees everyone who enters the passage and the stair. From **15's front window** he sees Vrba Street in both directions and the tram stop. From the **third-floor gallery rail** he looks straight down onto the second-floor gallery and Marta's door at 14, and across the courtyard well onto 12's kitchen window. The stair has an open well: a light switched on at any landing is visible from the top. Nobody reaches 14 without crossing two of these lines. The only blind spot is the cellar hatch under the chestnut, which 16 overlooks and 15 does not. [D]

## The medicine route [D]

Tuesdays. Pavel Dusek, in Vienna, buys a Western inhaler (blue plastic; the Lom pharmacy lists it and has never stocked it) and gives it to a sleeping-car attendant on the international train. The train is inspected on the Brenava bridge at **14:10** by the 4th Battalion — Tomas's company on alternate weeks — where a sealed parcel in the staff compartment is or is not opened. The train reaches Kalenov Main Station at 16:40; the attendant passes the parcel across the kiosk counter to **Eva Novak**, who brings it home on tram 11 and up the stair, past the lodge, to 14, by 17:30. Marta books the **Vienna call** at Lom post office for 18:00 the same evening, three minutes. If the call is missed, Pavel's next letter says "the weather here is poor", which means the parcel was taken.

The inhaler is the dossier use: it is smuggled medicine, it implicates Eva, the attendant and the garrison, and it is the one thing in the house that can be bargained.

## The building at 21:10 [K]

The music programme has been on ten minutes. Through walls: three tube radios slightly out of tune with each other; the Novaks' argument about the depot; Ilona's pen on the kitchen table at 12; Marta's cough in short sets from 14; Sedlak's radio loudest because of the ear. The stair light clicks off; someone presses it again; it clicks off. On the gallery, washing taken in. At 21:30 Kastan's gate round: keys, the gate bar, his step on each landing, a pause outside 13. The courtyard sodium lamp (state light) comes on at dusk; the gallery bulbs (private light) go off one by one.

## The building at 3 a.m. [K]

Nothing from the street until the first tram at 4:30. The gutter drips into the courtyard drain. Cast-iron stoves tick as they cool. Marta's breathing, audible on the gallery. Occasionally the telephone in 15 rings once and stops. [D] A car with its lights off has parked at the tram loop twice this week. [H] It is the recorder room's van, testing the line to 12.
