## The house [K]

A four-storey stucco **gallery house** built in 1894 under the old Crown, in the Lom quarter. Street front: five windows wide, ochre stucco gone grey-green, a carriage passage with a wooden gate that is locked at 22:00 and opened by the caretaker. Behind it, a cobbled **courtyard** with one chestnut, the bins, a carpet-beating rail, and a cellar hatch. The upper floors are reached by one **stair** in the rear corner of the passage, and each floor opens onto an **open gallery** (*pavlac*) that runs along the courtyard side; rear flats are entered from the gallery, front flats from the landing.

Six flats, numbered 1–6, two to a floor: odd numbers are the rear flats, entered from the gallery; even numbers are the front flats, entered from the landing. The ground floor is unnumbered. Ground floor now: the carriage passage, the caretaker's **lodge** (a cubby with a hatch window onto the passage, by day Kastan's post), a closed cooperative repair shop, the cellar.

## Sightlines [K]

Kastan has three. From the **lodge hatch** he sees everyone who enters the passage and the stair. From **6's front window** he sees Vrba Street in both directions and the tram stop. From the **third-floor gallery rail** he looks straight down onto the second-floor gallery and Marta's door at 3, and along his own gallery to 5's door and kitchen window. The stair has an open well: a light switched on at any landing is visible from the top. Nobody reaches 3 without crossing two of these lines. The only blind spot is the cellar hatch under the chestnut, which 1 overlooks and 6 does not. [D]

## The medicine route [D]

Tuesdays. Marta's son, in Vienna, buys a Western inhaler (blue plastic; the Lom pharmacy lists it and has never stocked it) and gives it to a sleeping-car attendant on the international train, **Marek**. The train is inspected on the Brenava bridge at **14:10** by the 4th Battalion — Tomas's company on alternate weeks — where a sealed parcel in the staff compartment is or is not opened. The train reaches Kalenov Main Station at 16:40; Marek carries the parcel himself, on tram 11 and up the stair, past the lodge, to 3, by 17:30. This is what Kastan reported as 4471. Marta books the **Vienna call** at Lom post office for 18:00 the same evening, three minutes. If the call is missed, her son's next letter says "the weather here is poor", which means the parcel was taken.

The inhaler is the dossier use: it is smuggled medicine, it implicates the attendant and the garrison, and it is the one thing in the house that can be bargained.

## The building at 21:10 [K]

The music programme has been on ten minutes. Through walls: three tube radios slightly out of tune with each other; the Novaks' argument about the depot; Ilona's pen on the kitchen table at 5; Marta's cough in short sets from 3; Sedlak's radio loudest because of the ear. The stair light clicks off; someone presses it again; it clicks off. On the gallery, washing taken in. At 21:30 Kastan's gate round: keys, the gate bar, his step on each landing, a pause outside 4. The courtyard sodium lamp (state light) comes on at dusk; the gallery bulbs (private light) go off one by one.

## The building at 3 a.m. [K]

Nothing from the street until the first tram at 4:30. The gutter drips into the courtyard drain. Cast-iron stoves tick as they cool. Marta's breathing, audible on the gallery. Occasionally the telephone in 6 rings once and stops. [D] A car with its lights off has parked at the tram loop twice this week. [H] It is the recorder room's van, testing the line to 5.
